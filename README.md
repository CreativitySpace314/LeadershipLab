# The Leadership Lab

**Practice the human side of leadership.**

The Leadership Lab is a public, interactive practice space for courageous, relational leadership. It is designed around a simple principle: **people aren't experiments; leadership is the practice.**

## First build

- Interactive homepage
- The Human in Front of You branching reflection
- The Mirror
- The Pause
- The Stories We Tell
- Values in Action
- The Conversation
- Repair
- A private browser-based Lab Notebook using localStorage
- No accounts or sign-in required

## Philosophy

The site focuses on curiosity, courage, accountability, connection, self-awareness, and repair. Experiences are designed for reflection rather than scoring people as having chosen a single universally "correct" response.
