BUILT FOR NEW SUPERVISORS

Leadership gets real
before you feel ready.

The first write-up. A staff member crying. Someone challenging you in front of the team. A medication error. Real responsibility has a way of making something inside us get very loud.

Leadership Lab helps you notice that internal reaction, practice what you might actually say, and see how your response changes the room. No grades. No perfect scripts. No pretending supervisors always get it right.

SO... WHAT IS THIS?

A place to look at yourself before you look at your staff.

1

Notice what gets loud.

Urgency? Control? Approval? Curiosity? Courage? The self-check shows the patterns behind your choices.

2

Try the actual words.

Type what you would really say. The conversation shifts based on how you enter it.

3

Flip the script.

New information appears. Sometimes the staff member isn't the problem. Sometimes the supervisor is.
