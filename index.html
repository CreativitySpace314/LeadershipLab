FOR NEW SUPERVISORS

You got the title.
Now comes the human part.

A staff member cries during feedback. Somebody challenges you in front of the team. You are holding a write-up. A medication error gets reported. Suddenly your own reactions are in the room too.

Leadership Lab is where you practice those moments before they are live. You will start by noticing what gets loud inside you, then use that insight in conversations and supervisor modules.

THE WHOLE POINT

Do not start by fixing the staff member.

Start by noticing what you bring into the room. Then practice leading through the actual responsibility.

NEW SUPERVISOR POCKET TOOLS

Sometimes you need a tool, not a lecture.

These are short enough to use before a real conversation.

BEFORE CORRECTINGFacts before story +
FACT: What could a camera have seen or heard?
STORY: What meaning did you add?
IMPACT: What needs to be addressed?
QUESTION: What do you still need to understand?
“Here is what I am seeing, here is the impact, and I want to hear what I may be missing.”
WHEN YOU FEEL DEFENSIVEThe 10-second reset +
Before explaining yourself, ask: Am I trying to understand, or am I trying to prove I am right?
One useful move: “Say more. I want to make sure I understand what you are seeing.”
DIFFICULT FEEDBACKHuman + clear +
1. Name what happened.
2. Name why it matters.
3. Invite context.
4. Make the next expectation visible.
Compassion does not require vagueness. Accountability does not require humiliation.
WHEN YOU MISSEDRepair without defending +
Own the behavior before explaining the intent. Ask about impact. Then name what you will do differently.
“I want to come back to how I handled that. I cut you off, and I do not think that was okay. What was that moment like from your side?”
WHERE THIS IS GOING

Real supervisor modules.

The med-error preview is the model: a real event happens, your internal reaction shows up, you make a choice, new information appears, and you lead the next move. Policy tells you what must happen. The Lab helps you practice how you show up while it happens.
