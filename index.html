HOW THE LAB WORKS

One loop. Again and again.

The goal isn't to memorize the right line. It's to notice yourself, see the effect of your response, and practice a better next move.

1

Establish your baseline.

Notice what gets loud inside you when responsibility, conflict, uncertainty, or power enters the room.

2

Observe the pattern.

The Lab shows the choices that shaped your readout. No mystery score and no leadership personality box.

3

Put it under pressure.

Type what you would actually say. Watch what your response makes easier or harder for another human to tell you.

4

Adjust and run it again.

Use what you learned in live simulations and real supervisor tools. Leadership is the variable you can change.

FIELD KIT

Take the Lab with you.

Short tools for the five minutes before a real conversation.

STORY SCANNERFacts before story +
FACT: What could a camera have seen or heard?
STORY: What meaning did you add?
IMPACT: What needs to be addressed?
QUESTION: What do you still need to understand?
“Here is what I am seeing, here is the impact, and I want to hear what I may be missing.”
ARMOR ALERTThe 10-second reset +
Before explaining yourself, ask: Am I trying to understand, or am I trying to prove I am right?
“Say more. I want to make sure I understand what you are seeing.”
CLARITY CHECKHuman + clear +
1. Name what happened.
2. Name why it matters.
3. Invite context.
4. Make the next expectation visible.
Compassion does not require vagueness. Accountability does not require humiliation.
REPAIR KITRepair without defending +
Own the behavior before explaining the intent. Ask about impact. Then name what you will do differently.
“I want to come back to how I handled that. I cut you off, and I don't think that was okay. What was that moment like from your side?”
